---
title: Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets
category: Economy
order: 15
---
**Home** starts with what is waiting for you today.

**Where you claim.** Home has a side bar, **To claim**. From the top: your sealed packs with an
**Open** button, the daily check-in, the free pack, today's goals, your collector level, the
milestone row and the Getting-started list. A claim changes only the side bar; the rest of the
page stays where it is. In a narrow window the side bar sits below the page. Every reward is a
sealed pack, never coins.

**What is still missing.** The line under the progress bar on Home says what is left to do: how
many cards are still in the packs, how many of your cards need tags and, once they are all tagged,
how many are still Normal ("31 of your cards are still Normal: merge copies or pull a rarer one").
When you have no sealed pack and too few coins, it also says how many coins you are short
("82 more coins for a Standard Pack"). It ends with your versions: "You hold 187 of 720 versions (26%)."

**The main button** on Home is the first of these that applies: **Open a pack** (you hold a sealed
one), **Buy a pack** (you can afford one), **Fill versions · N cards** (it opens the Collection's
Missing-versions list), then tagging, selling spare copies and how to earn coins.

**Happening now.** A strip on Home lists what has a clock on it: a collector's offer that waits in
your messages, the shop's deal, your tag rush, cards that collectors want, and what your collection
is worth with the time until prices change next. Click an entry to go there. The strip is never
empty: when nothing else has a clock, it points at your free pack or your check-in, or it counts
down to the next price update.

**Missing one version.** A tile on Home lists up to 10 of your cards that lack exactly one of Foil,
Holo and Special, the most valuable first, each with the treatment that is missing. Click a card to
open its panel. The tile is left out while no card is one version short.
See [What are versions, and what is the ring on a card?](#help:versions-and-ring)

**Messages.** The **✉** button in the top bar opens your messages; a number on it counts the new
ones. An offer from a collector slides in as a small message and waits there until you take it or
turn it down. The shop's deal, wanted cards, a tag rush that is ready and news about your own cards
are there too.

**Daily check-in.** Check in once a day. It pays no coins: it keeps your streak (which starts
over if you miss a day) and unlocks the free pack.

**Free pack.** After today's check-in you can claim a free Standard pack, once a day. It goes
into **Your packs**, sealed, and you open it when you want. It costs no coins and does not raise
pack prices. You can also claim it on **Packs**. When it is ready and you have no sealed pack, the
**Packs** button in the bottom bar is marked **free**.

**Today's goals.** Once you have bought 5 packs (the free pack does not count), Home shows three
small goals every day, in the side bar under the check-in and the free pack. **Stats** shows them too. Each goal has a bar and
a reward; when the bar is full, press **Claim**. New goals come at midnight, your local time. A goal
you did not reach or did not claim is gone then. The three are picked from this list:

- Open 2 packs
- Open 5 packs
- Pull a Foil-or-better card
- Fuse 1 card
- Fuse copies into a rarer card (one fuse does it)
- Sell 3 copies on the market
- Earn 30 coins on the market
- Add 5 tags (only tags that pay count)
- Review 1 shinier card
- Buy a card on the market

Each goal pays **1 Standard pack**, sealed, into **Your packs**.

- **You only get goals you can work on.** When every tag on your cards is paid out, you get no tag
  goal. When you can't afford the cheapest card on the market, you get no buy goal.
- **The goals of a day don't change** once you have seen them. The same goes for the week.
- Most of them need no untagged cards, so there are goals when every card is tagged too.
- **"Fuse" in a goal means merge.** One merge in a card's panel does "Fuse 1 card".

**Collector level.** Under today's goals is your collector level with a bar to the next one. It
rises when your cards get rarer and when you merge copies; Normal cards give nothing. Each level pays a pack
once: press **Claim** when one is reached. See [What is my collector level?](#help:collector-level)

**Your next goal.** A tile at the bottom of Home always names one goal: how far it is and what it
pays. It is the nearest one among today's goals, this week's goals, your next collector
level, the next milestone and your nearest set. A goal that only needs claiming comes first. Click
the tile to go where the goal is worked on.

**The shop deal.** Every 4 hours one pack is 20% to 40% cheaper, for at most two packs.

**Tag rush.** Once a day, press **⚡ Start the tag rush** in **Organize**: every tag pays double
for 10 minutes. Afterwards Organize says when the next one is ready (the next day, and at least
8 hours after the last one started). See [Tagging and categories](#help:tagging).

**The market.** Card prices change every 6 hours, and collectors make a few offers a day for your
best cards. See [The card market](#help:card-market) and
[When do prices change?](#help:when-do-prices-change)

**Milestones.** Each time your collection completeness passes a step, a milestone waits to be
claimed. There are 15 steps: 1%, 2%, 5%, then every 5% up to 50%, then 60%, 75% and 100%. They pay
sealed packs, from 1 Standard pack at the start to 5 Premium packs at 100%. Nothing is paid while
a pack is opened: a message says "Milestone 5% reached: 1 Standard pack to claim", and you press
**Claim** on the milestone row under your collector level, in Home's side bar or on **Stats**.
While none waits, that row names the next one ("Next milestone 10%"). Home and **Stats** show how far the next one is.
Completeness counts the Foil, Holo and Special versions you hold, three for every card, so packs
and merging raise it. Tags and Normal copies don't. Each step pays once: a step you reached stays yours to claim when your completeness falls, and
passing it again does not pay again. A step is counted against the largest library you ever had:
if files leave your folder, the percentage you see goes up, but the steps stay where they were,
and the screens then say how many more versions the next step needs. See [How is completeness counted?](#help:completeness)

**Weekly goals.** Once you have bought 30 packs, three bigger goals appear every Monday on
**Stats**. Each pays 1 Deluxe pack. Fill a goal's bar, then press
**Claim**. The list: pull 10 Foil-or-better cards, pull 2 Holo-or-better cards, fuse copies into
a rarer card 3 times, apply 120 tags (only when that many tags still pay in your library), review 8 shinier cards, open 15 packs,
fuse 5 cards, sell 15 copies on the market, earn 300 coins on the market. The last three need no
untagged cards.

**Sets.** A tag with 5 or more cards is a set. Own every card of a set in Foil (or better) to
claim its Foil reward, then again for Holo and for Special. **Stats** lists your sets with the
ones closest to a reward first.
- A set pays a pack: Standard for Foil, Deluxe for Holo, Premium for Special, and two of them for a
  set of 15 cards or more.
- A card counts toward set rewards once per treatment. Putting a second tag on cards that
  already counted does not pay again.
- Cards whose file is missing don't count.

**The same odds for every pack.** What you opened before does not change a pack's odds. See [Packs and rarities](#help:packs-and-rarities).

**Stats** also keeps your latest packs and your near misses (rolls that landed just short of the
next treatment).
