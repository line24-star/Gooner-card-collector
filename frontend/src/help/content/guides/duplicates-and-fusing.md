title: Duplicates and merging
category: Economy
order: 25

A **duplicate** is a card you pull from a pack although you already own it in that treatment. The
reveal says **Duplicate** under the card.

**A duplicate is kept.** It becomes an extra copy of the card. It pays no coins by itself and it
changes nothing by itself. Under a card in your Collection you see the copies you hold per
treatment: "2 Normal", or "2 Normal · 1 Foil". What you do with the extra copies is up to you:

**Sell them.** Open the card and look at **Your versions**. Each treatment has its own row with a
miniature of the card, and a row you hold says what one copy sells for today.
- **Sell 1** sells one copy.
- **Sell N extras** sells all but one copy of that treatment.
- On the **Market** page, **Sell all duplicates** does this for every card at once.

The market pays 85% of a card's price, the same for every copy: selling does not lower the price.
See [The card market](#help:card-market).

**Merge them.** Several copies of one card in the same treatment merge into **one copy of the next
treatment**. For most cards that takes three copies:

| You give | You get |
|---|---|
| 3 Normal copies | 1 Foil copy |
| 3 Foil copies | 1 Holo copy |
| 3 Holo copies | 1 Special copy |

Each row under **Your versions** says how far you are, with a small bar: "2 of 3 for a Foil". When
you hold enough, a **Merge 3 → Foil** button appears in that row. Merging costs no coins and always
works. Special is the highest treatment and cannot be merged further. If the card was already
tagged, the new rarer copy can earn a **Review** bonus.

**"Merge" and "fuse" are the same thing.** The buttons say merge. The goals still say fuse
("Fuse 1 card"): one merge does it.

**How many copies a merge takes.** A card that is worth more takes more copies. What counts is the
market price of a **Normal** copy of that card today:

| A Normal copy is worth | Copies for one merge |
|---|---|
| under 20 coins | 3 |
| 20 to 59 coins | 4 |
| 60 to 179 coins | 5 |
| 180 to 539 coins | 6 |
| 540 coins or more | 7 |

- The number is the same for every treatment of the card: a card that takes 5 Normal copies for a
  Foil also takes 5 Foils for a Holo.
- About 7 in 10 cards take 3. Only the most valuable 1 in 100 take 6 or 7.
- The row and the button show the card's own number ("4 of 5 for a Foil", "Merge 5 → Foil"). When
  it is above 3, the note under **Your versions** says so.
- **The number follows the market.** Prices change every 6 hours. When a card's price climbs past
  a step, a merge takes one more copy from then on. When the price drops back, it takes one fewer.
- **Your own trades don't change it.** They do not move a price at all.
- Merging is the one way to make a card rarer yourself. There are no upgrade attempts any more.

**The app tells you when you can merge.**
- **Right after a pack:** when a pulled card brings you to enough copies, a button
  **Merge 3 Normal → Foil: (card name)** appears under the results, with the number that card takes.
  One tap merges them.
- **In the card panel:** a line on top of the left half says "You hold 3 Normal copies: merge 3
  into a rarer one."

**A merge that empties a version slot asks first.** A version counts only while you hold a copy of
it. When a merge would use up your last copies of a Foil or a Holo, a dialog opens before anything
happens. It lists:
- what you give up ("3 Foil copies, worth 🪙 84 on the market"),
- what you get ("1 Holo copy"),
- the real change in versions: **+1**, **0** or **−1**,
- the slot that empties ("This will empty the Foil slot").

The cases:
- Merging Normal copies into a Foil never empties a slot: Normal is not a version. It is +1 when
  you held no Foil, and 0 when you did.
- Merging your last 3 Foils into your first Holo empties the Foil slot and fills the Holo slot: 0.
- Merging your last 3 Foils when you already hold a Holo empties the Foil slot and fills nothing: −1.
- To keep both, merge when you hold one copy more than the merge takes: with 4 Foils, a merge of 3
  leaves 1 Foil and gives 1 Holo: +1.

After a merge the card panel stays open, and the ring on the card and the pill in the top bar show
the change.

**What a merge counts for**
- **Goals.** The daily goals "Fuse 1 card" and "Fuse copies into a rarer card", and the weekly goals
  "Fuse 5 cards" and "Fuse copies into a rarer card 3 times". See [Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets](#help:daily-and-goals).
- **Your collector level.** Every merge gives 2 points, and the card gives more points when its best
  copy gets rarer. See [What is my collector level?](#help:collector-level)
- **Versions and completeness.** A merge fills the version it makes, if you did not hold it yet. A
  milestone you pass pays at once. See [How is completeness counted?](#help:completeness)

**Sell or merge?** Merging fills versions (see above) and makes every later tag on that card pay
more. Selling gives you coins now. A scarce card's extra copies can be worth a lot, and a valuable
card takes more copies to merge, so check the price before you merge them.

**Keep one.** If you sell your last copy of a card, the card leaves your collection until you pull
it again. Selling your last copy of a Foil, Holo or Special empties that version slot. The app
asks before either happens and says what you lose. **Sell N extras** and **Sell all duplicates**
keep one copy of each treatment, so they never empty a slot.
