A quick path from an empty app to your first rare card.

1. **Set up.** The first time you open the app, the setup screen shows your media folder. Press
   **Choose folder…** to pick the folder with your photos and videos. It is scanned right away and
   the count goes up. Press **Start playing** when it does. See [Adding media](#help:adding-media).
2. **Buy and open your first pack.** You start with 500 welcome coins: five Standard packs. On
   **Home**, the big button takes you to the shop. Press **Buy 1**, then **Open** under **Your packs**,
   pick one of the three packs and flip your cards. A face-down card with a green edge may fill one
   of your versions: a hint that is usually right, not always.
   [See the packs page](#tour:packs)
3. **Check in.** Everything you can claim is in the side bar of **Home**, under **To claim** (below
   the page in a narrow window). Press **Claim** on the daily check-in, then claim your **free pack**.
   It lands in your packs, sealed; the first row of the side bar counts them and has an **Open** button.
4. **Tag what you pulled.** Open **Organize** and tag your cards. Type a tag and press **Enter**,
   or press **1** to **9** for one of your most used tags. Every tag earns coins, and rarer cards
   pay more. A wrong tag? Click it to remove it. [Take the Organize tour](#tour:organize)
5. **Follow the checklist.** The side bar of **Home** has a **Getting started** list. Tagging your first five cards
   and your first merge each pay a Standard pack when you press **Claim**. If you hide it, your **Player** page can bring it back.
6. **Do today's goals.** Once you have bought 5 packs, the side bar of **Home** shows three small
   goals every day. Each pays a Standard pack when you press **Claim**. See
   [Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets](#help:daily-and-goals).
7. **Buy more packs** with the coins you earned. Coins come from tagging and from new files;
   one tag on each new card of a pack pays for about the next Standard pack.
8. **Merge copies into a rarer card.** When you hold three copies of a card in one treatment, its
   panel has a **Merge 3 → …** button. See [The card panel: versions, selling, merging, price](#help:upgrading-and-selling).
9. **Look at the market.** Every card has a value, and a card you pull twice is kept as an extra
   copy. Sell extra copies on the **Market** page, or merge three into a rarer one (a valuable card takes more).
   See [The card market](#help:card-market) and [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
10. **Keep an eye on the top bar.** It shows your coins, your versions ("12 / 720 versions": the
   Foil, Holo and Special copies you hold, of three for every card; click it for the cards with a
   missing version), and what your collection is worth, with ▲ or ▼ and how much it moved at the last price update. The **✉** button
   holds your messages: when a collector makes an offer for one of your cards, it slides in and
   waits there.

Taking all five tours to their last step pays one more Standard pack; a message says so when it happens.
The line under the checklist counts them ("3 of 5 tours taken").
See [How do I earn coins?](#help:earning-coins) for the rest.
