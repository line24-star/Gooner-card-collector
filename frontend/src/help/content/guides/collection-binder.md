**Collection** shows the cards you **pulled from packs**, two ways. A photo or video you have not
pulled yet is still in a pack and is not shown; the counter says how many of all your cards you have.

- **Grid:** everything in one fast, scrolling grid that uses the whole width of the window. It
  stays smooth with thousands of cards. The slider in the toolbar makes the cards smaller or larger.
- **Binder:** pages of pockets, like a real card album: 3 × 3 pockets a page, so 18 cards on the two open pages. The slider makes the pockets smaller or larger too, as far as both pages fit the window. Turn pages with **A / D**, or jump ten pages with **Q / E**.
- **Binder, Versions:** press **Versions** next to the page buttons. Each spread then shows one
  card in four pockets: Normal and Foil on the left page, Holo and Special on the right. A version
  you do not hold is a dashed frame that says "Not owned"; click it to see a preview of that version
  full screen. **A / D** turn one card, **Q / E** jump ten.

**What a card shows in the grid**
- **The version ring** on the card: three segments for Foil, Holo and Special. A thick coloured
  segment is a version you hold, a thin outline is one you miss.
  See [What are versions, and what is the ring on a card?](#help:versions-and-ring)
- **Its value** under the card: what your best copy is worth on the market, ▲ or ▼ when the price
  moved over the last 24 hours, ★ when a collector wants the card.
- **Your copies**, when you hold more than one: "2 Normal", or "2 Normal · 1 Foil" when you hold
  copies in several treatments. Under a small card the names shrink to letters ("2 N · 1 F · 1 H").
- **A price-move badge** ("▲ 11%" or "▼ 9%") when you hold exactly one of the card's Foil, Holo
  and Special and its price moved more than 3% over the last 24 hours. Click it to open the card with
  the price in view; its × hides it until the next price update.

The counter at the top adds up what the whole collection is worth. **Sort** orders the cards by
the order you pulled them, by value, by rarity, by number of copies, by name or by fewest missing
versions. **Mergeable first** puts the cards on top that have enough copies for a merge right now,
the most valuable first.

**Filters.** The chips show **All** cards, only **Untagged** cards (the fastest way to earn coins),
the cards with **Missing versions**, or the cards you own in a single treatment.

**Missing versions** lists the cards that lack a Foil, a Holo or a Special. The cards closest to
holding all three come first, then the most valuable. The line above the list says how many there
are and links to the packs (**Open packs to fill versions**). The version pill in the top bar and
the **Fill versions** button on Home open this view. When you came that way and sell or merge in a
card's panel, the panel closes and you are back on this list.

**Card names.** A card is called "Card" and its number, such as "Card #000412", until you name it. Every card has such a six-digit number. To
give a card your own name, open it and press **✎** next to the name.
See [Why does my card have a different name than the file?](#help:card-names)

**The card panel.** Click any card (or focus it and press **Enter**) to open its panel. On the left
are your versions and the tags, on the right the price. Click the large card in the panel to see
it **full screen**; click beside it or press **Esc** to close. A video card plays inside its frame.
Move your pointer over a card to tilt it and see the foil and holo shine.
See [The card panel: versions, selling, merging, price](#help:upgrading-and-selling).

The **Tags** button opens a tag and category browser, and **Select** lets you tag several cards at once. Bulk tags pay a quarter of what a tag pays in Organize, and at least 1 coin. See [Tagging and categories](#help:tagging) and [The card market](#help:card-market).
