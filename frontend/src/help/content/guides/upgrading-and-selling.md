---
title: The card panel: versions, selling, merging, price
category: Organizing
order: 20
---
Click any card to open its panel: in the **Collection**, on **Home**, on the **Market**, in the
news, or a card you just turned in a pack.
[Show me the panel](#tour:card-panel)

The panel has a header, the large card, and two halves. **Left:** your versions and the tags.
**Right:** the price. In a narrow window they sit under each other.

**Name and number.** The header shows the card's name and its permanent number ("#000412"). Press
**✎** next to the name to give the card your own name (up to 60 characters). Leave the field empty
to get the automatic name back. Only the card is renamed: your file keeps its name.
See [Why does my card have a different name than the file?](#help:card-names)

**Value.** Under the name you see what your best copy is worth on the market today, how the
price moved and over what time ("▼ 3% in 24 h", "▲ 12% this month": the range the chart on the
right shows), and how scarce the card is (its print tier and print run). "Only in Premium
Packs" means the cheaper packs don't hold this card. "Wanted" means it sells for more right now.
The line under it says what your last action did ("Sold 1 Foil copy for 31 coins · −1 version").

**The large card.** Click it to see the card **full screen**. Click beside it or press **Esc** to
come back.

**What to do next.** On top of the left half, one line (➜) names the best thing to do with this
card right now: merge it when you hold enough copies of one treatment, sell it when a collector
wants it, or add its first tag. When none of these applies, there is no line.

**Your versions.** Four rows: Normal, Foil, Holo and Special. Each has a miniature of the card in
that treatment's look. **Click a miniature to see that version full screen**, also one you do not
hold: that is a preview, marked "Not owned". Such a miniature says **NOT OWNED**, and the link
**Not owned · preview** next to it opens the same preview.

- **A version you hold** shows the number of copies ("×2"), what one copy sells for today, and how
  far you are from a merge: **"2 of 3 for a Foil"** with a small bar. The number is the card's own:
  a valuable card takes more than 3.
  - **Sell 1** sells one copy on the market. **Sell N extras** sells all but one. The market pays
    85% of the card's price, the same for every copy: selling does not lower the price. The button
    names what the sale pays.
  - **Merge 3 → Foil** appears when you hold enough copies. It turns them into one copy of the next
    treatment. It costs nothing and always works. A card that is worth more takes more copies: 4
    from a Normal price of 20 coins, 5 from 60, 6 from 180 and 7 from 540. If a merge is refused,
    the panel says why and shows the card as it is now.
    See [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
- **A version you do not hold** is dimmed and says **Not owned**, what a copy is worth on the
  market, and how often each pack gives that treatment ("Standard Pack: 1 in 13 packs").
  **Find in packs** takes you to the shop.

**The app asks only when you would lose something.** A sale or a merge is one tap. A dialog inside
the app opens first in these cases:
- You sell your last copy of a Foil, Holo or Special: "This will empty the Foil slot (−1 version)".
- You sell your last copy of the card: it leaves your collection until you pull it again.
- A merge uses up your last copies of a Foil or Holo: the dialog lists what you give up, what you
  get and the real change in versions (+1, 0 or −1).

The dialog shows what you receive next to what you lose. **Enter** goes ahead, **Esc** keeps the
card. Afterwards the panel stays open; the ring on the card and the pill in the top bar show the
change.

**Tags.** Under your versions. The line **"The next tag pays +N coins"** is above the field, then
you type a tag and press **Enter**. **Click a tag to remove it:** the coins it paid are taken
back, and the line under the value says how many ("Removed "beach" · −30 coins"). See
[Tagging and categories](#help:tagging).

**Review.** If you tagged a card and later get a shinier version of it, a **Review · +N**
button appears with the tags, showing the exact bonus. It pays once per card and rarity. The bonus
only applies to a version you got after the card was first tagged. A version you already had when
you tagged it does not qualify, because tagging it already paid the full rarity multiplier.

**Price.** The right half. Pick the version it is about: **Normal**, **Foil**, **Holo** or
**Special** (your best copy at first). You see what a copy of it is worth today, a chart of the
price over **24 h**, a **Week** or a **Month**, and a table of **the last 30 days**: the date, the
price at the end of the day and the change against the day before. The change next to the price
is the one of the range you picked, and it says so ("▲ 4% in 24 h", "▲ 12% this week").

**A card you do not hold** opens too, from the news or from a market listing. Every version then
says "Not owned", and the price half works as usual.

**No upgrade attempts.** Earlier versions had a **Try upgrade** button that paid coins for a
chance to move a card up one rarity. It is switched off since version 0.7.1: upgrading a card to
Special and selling it paid far more than it cost. Merging is the one way to make a card rarer
yourself.

Your coin total in the top bar always shows the server's number. For prices and selling, see
[The card market](#help:card-market). For how rarities and packs work, see
[Packs and rarities](#help:packs-and-rarities).
