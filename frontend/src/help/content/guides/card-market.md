Every card has a **market value**. The **Market** page shows what your cards are worth, buys your
copies and sells the most valuable cards of each treatment. The 📈 number in the top bar is what
your whole collection is worth today. Next to it, ▲ or ▼ and a percentage show how it moved at the
last price update.
[Take the Market tour](#tour:market)

**What makes a card valuable**
- **Scarcity.** When a card is scanned in, it gets a **print run**: how many copies of it exist.
  The fewer copies, the more the card is worth, and the less often a pack pulls it.
- **Demand.** Some cards are simply more wanted than others. Demand counts for more on scarce
  cards: a wanted common card is worth a little more, a wanted scarce card many times more.
- **Treatment.** A rarer copy is worth more, and how much more is the card's own: a Foil copy is
  worth 3 to 4 times a Normal copy of the same card, a Holo 8.3 to 14 times and a Special 23 to
  50 times. The card panel shows the price of every version, also the ones you do not hold.

So an expensive card does not have to be shiny. A plain Normal copy of a scarce card can be worth
more than a Foil of a common one.

**A few cards are worth far more than the rest**, as in a real card set. About half of your cards
are worth around 10 coins. A scarce card that is also wanted can be worth thousands. In a library
of 2,000 cards the most valuable one is typically worth about 470 times a typical card.

A card's panel shows its print tier and print run:

| Print tier | Share of your cards | Copies printed |
|---|---|---|
| Mass print | 50% | 20,000 to 60,000 |
| Standard print | 30% | 5,000 to 15,000 |
| Short print | 14% | 1,000 to 3,000 |
| Limited | 5% | 200 to 600 |
| Chase | 1% | 25 to 100 |

**Prices change every 6 hours**, for all cards at the same moment, also while the app is closed.
The Market page counts down to the next update and lists the four times of day. Between two
updates no price moves.
See [When do prices change?](#help:when-do-prices-change)
- Prices rise and fall in long waves, and every card also moves a little by itself.
- Now and then a card is hyped and its price jumps, or someone buys up a scarce card.
- **A jump has momentum.** After a hype or a buyout the card is on a run for a few updates. At
  each one it rises again (6 times in 10, in smaller steps: +15%, then +9%, then +5% after a
  typical hype) or the run breaks and the price drops back a good part (−22.5% after a typical
  hype, −43% after a typical buyout). Rise and drop cancel out on average, so a run is a fair bet,
  not a sure gain.
- About once a month the most valuable cards are **reprinted**: more copies exist, so they are
  worth less and easier to pull. Cards printed fewer than 150 times are never reprinted.
- **Market news** at the bottom of the page lists what happened. Each line names the cards it is
  about, your own first. Every name is a link: click it to open that card's panel, also for a card
  you do not hold.

**Your own trades do not move a price.** You are one collector among millions. Selling ten copies
pays ten times what one copy pays, and a card costs the same after you bought it as before.
(Before version 0.8.0 every copy you sold lowered the price by 3%.)

**Your collection.** The top of the Market page is about your own cards: what your collection is
worth, how much it went up or down at the last update, and a chart of what the cards you hold now
were worth over the last month. The value counts the cards you hold now, so it also changes when
you open packs, sell or buy. **Your cards that moved most** lists the cards of yours whose price
changed most over the last 24 hours ("▲ 12% in 24 h").

**Selling.** Under **Your cards**, each card has a **Sell** button for every treatment you own.
The market pays **85%** of the card's price. A sale goes through at once. The app asks first only
when the sale loses something:
- your last copy of a Foil, Holo or Special: "This will empty the Foil slot (−1 version)";
- your last copy of the card: it leaves your collection until you pull it again.

The question is a dialog inside the app that shows what you receive next to what you lose.
**Sell all duplicates** sells every extra copy and keeps one of each card and treatment, so no
version slot empties. It always asks, and names the coins.

**Wanted cards.** Collectors want up to three of your cards at a time. Those sell for **30% more**
until the market moves again. They are marked with a ★, and the line says what that is in coins:
"Your Foil copy sells for 🪙 31 instead of 24". A card is only wanted when your copy is worth 20
coins or more, so the bonus is worth having. When you pull a wanted card from a pack, a prompt
under the results says the same and opens the card's panel, where you sell it.

**Goals on the market.** Some daily and weekly goals are done here: sell copies, earn coins on the
market, buy a card. A collector's offer you take counts as a sale.
See [Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets](#help:daily-and-goals).

**Offers from collectors.** A few times a day a collector makes an offer for one of your five most
valuable copies: between 10% below and 12% above its market price. A normal sale pays 85%, so an
offer is always better. A collector never makes an offer for a card that is wanted, because
selling a wanted card pays more, and never for a copy worth less than 20 coins. A new offer slides
in as a small message: press **Take it**, **Turn it down** or **Later**. It then waits behind the
**✉** button in the top bar until you answer it. An offer ends when prices change next.
- When the offer is for your only copy of a Foil, Holo or Special, it warns: "Accepting empties
  the Foil slot (−1 version)". When it is your only copy of the card, it says the card leaves your
  collection. In those two cases the app asks once more before the card goes.

**Messages.** The **✉** button in the top bar opens your messages; a number on it counts the new
ones. Besides offers you find there: cards that collectors want, the shop's deal, a tag rush that is
ready, and news about your own cards. When something new waits, the button grows and says what it
is and how long it stays ("Deal · 6 min 53 s"). It turns red for an offer and for anything that
ends within the hour.

**Buying.** Under **For sale** are four lists, one for each treatment: the **10 most valuable
Normal, Foil, Holo and Special cards**.
- Only cards whose Normal copy is worth **20 coins or more** are traded. In a small library a list
  can be short or empty; it says so.
- A card costs **115%** of the price of that treatment.
- **Buying gives you a copy of that treatment**, even if you never pulled the card. A Foil, Holo
  or Special copy fills its version slot when it was empty: the listing says "✚ Fills your Holo
  slot", and the pill in the top bar goes up by one.
- Each listing shows how many copies of that treatment you hold already.
- Buying does not raise the price.
- Click a listed card to open its panel, also when you do not hold it.

**Price history.** Open a card. The right half of its panel is about the price: pick **Normal**,
**Foil**, **Holo** or **Special**, see the chart over **24 h**, a **Week** or a **Month**, and
under it a table of **the last 30 days** with the price at the end of each day and the change
against the day before.

**A change always names its time.** "▼ 3% in 24 h", "▲ 12% this week", "▲ 12% this month": the
percentage in the card's panel is the change over the range the chart shows, so the number and
the line agree. A card can be down over 24 hours and up over the month. On the Market page and
in the Collection, ▲ and ▼ are the change over the last 24 hours.

**The price-move badge.** In the Collection, a card of which you hold exactly one of Foil, Holo
and Special gets a small badge when its price moved more than 3% over the last 24 hours: "▲ 11%" or
"▼ 9%". Click the badge to open the card with the price in view. Its × hides it for that card
until the next price update.
