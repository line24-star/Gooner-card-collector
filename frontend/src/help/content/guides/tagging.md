title: Tagging and categories
category: Organizing
order: 10
---
Tagging is how you organize your collection, and it is where most coins come from.
A tag is a short label such as `beach` or `family`. Tags are lowercase, extra spaces are
squeezed out, and they can be 1 to 40 characters long.

**One card at a time.** Open a card from **Collection** to see its panel. Under **Tags**, in the
left half, type a tag and press **Enter**. Add a **category** in the second box to file the tag under a group (a
`beach` tag under a `travel` category, for example). Click a tag to remove it: the coins it paid are taken back, and the panel says how many.
Above the tag field the panel says what the next tag on this card pays ("The next tag pays +5 coins").
[Show me the panel](#tour:card-panel)

**Suggestions while you type.** Click into a tag field and a list opens under it: tags that often
go together with the ones this card already has, and, once you type, the tags that match. Click one
to use it.

**Organize mode.** The **Organize** page shows your untagged cards one after another. Type a
tag and press **Enter**, or press **1** to **9** to use one of your nine most used tags.
**Tab** or **→** skips a card, **Esc** goes back to the Collection. Cards you own in a rarer
version come first because they pay the most. Click the card to see it full screen.
[Take the Organize tour](#tour:organize)

**A wrong tag?** Click it. In Organize and in the card panel a click on one of the card's tags
removes it and takes back the coins it paid ("Removed "beach" · −30 coins"). Add the right tag
and it pays again.

**Keep going when everything has a tag.** When no untagged card is left, Organize offers a batch
of **10, 20 or 50** cards that already have tags. Each batch is a new random pick from the cards
that still pay. In a batch the card stays after each tag, so you
can add several; **Tab** or **→** moves on to the next card. Above the tag field you see what the
next tag on this card pays. Between two batches the page shows your tag rush first, when it is
running or ready.

**Many cards at once.** In **Collection**, press **Select**, click the cards you want, type a
tag and press **Apply**. One bulk request handles up to 200 cards. Larger selections are sent
in several requests. **Bulk tags pay a quarter:** a tag placed on many cards at once pays 25% of
what the same tag pays in Organize or in the card panel, and never less than 1 coin for a tag
that pays. It is the fast way to sort cards, not the way to earn.

**Browsing by tag.** The **Tags** button in Collection opens a tree of your tags and categories
with a count for each. Click one to show only those cards. A category shows its own cards
and those of the tags directly under it. Click it again, or the × on the filter chip, to clear it.

**Coins.** The first tag on a card pays 30 coins, the next three pay 10, 5 and 2. Rarer cards
multiply that: Foil ×1.5, Holo ×2.5, Special ×3.5. After the fourth tag, every further tag pays
1 coin, up to 12 paid tags per card. More tags than that pay nothing, but they still help you find
the card. Tagging the same card with the same tag again never pays twice. Removing a tag takes back the
coins it paid; the next tag on that card pays again, at most what was taken back.
See [How do I earn coins?](#help:earning-coins)

**A short pause when you go very fast.** Tags are meant to describe the card. When quick tags
come very fast (seven paid ones within 8 seconds), the page asks for a breath: "Slow down: type
the next tag, or wait 4 s." The quick tags are off while the seconds count down. Type a tag and
the pause ends at once, and that tag is paid. A tag that still lands in the pause is put on the
card but pays nothing; the page tells you when that happens.

**Tag rush.** Once a day you can press **⚡ Start the tag rush** in Organize. For 10 minutes every
tag pays double. The clock keeps running, so start it when you have cards to tag. Afterwards
Organize says when the next one is ready: on the next day, and at least 8 hours after you
started the last one.

**Tags and card names.** Tags do not change a card's name. A card is called "Card" and its number,
such as "Card #000412", until you name it. You can give any card your own name in its panel.
See [Why does my card have a different name than the file?](#help:card-names)

If you see "Slow down a moment" instead, the app got too many requests at once. Wait a second and try again.
