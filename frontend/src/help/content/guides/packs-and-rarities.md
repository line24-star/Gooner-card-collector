**Every card comes out of a pack.** Each photo and video in your folder is a card, but you only
see it once you pulled it. The pill in the top bar counts the Foil, Holo and Special versions you
hold ("187 / 720 versions"); its tooltip says how many cards you have pulled so far.

Every card has a **treatment** that sets how it looks and how many coins it earns when you tag it:

| Treatment | Look |
|---|---|
| Normal | Classic card: title on top, your picture in a framed window, tags below |
| Foil | The same classic card with a metallic sheen that follows your pointer |
| Holo | **Full art**: your picture fills the whole card, with a rainbow holo and a glowing border |
| Special | **Full art** in a heavy gold frame, full holo and sparkles |

**The three packs.** They differ in the odds of a rare treatment, the number of cards and the
price. Better odds cost more per card:

| Pack | Cards | Price | A Foil in | A Holo in | A Special in |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 5 | 100 | 1 of 3 packs | 1 of 13 packs | 1 of 54 packs |
| Deluxe | 5 | 180 | 1 of 2 packs | 1 of 7 packs | 1 of 30 packs |
| Premium | 10 | 480 | 9 of 10 packs | 1 of 3 packs | 1 of 11 packs |

The Standard odds are modelled on real trading-card boosters. Each tile in the shop shows these
odds and today's price; **Odds per card, for every pack** under the tiles has the chance for each
single card.

**Some cards are only in the dearer packs.** Every card has a print run: how many copies of it
exist. The scarcest cards are not in the cheap packs. **Limited** cards are only in Deluxe and
Premium packs, **Chase** cards only in Premium packs. A pack's tile says how many such cards it
holds and how many you still miss. Scarce cards are also pulled less often than common ones.
See [Why can't I pull some cards from a Standard pack?](#help:pack-exclusives)

**Buying and opening are two steps**
1. In **Packs**, press **Buy 1** or **Buy 10**, or type a number next to **Buy**. The number can't
   go above what your coins buy. The packs go into **Your packs** at the top, sealed.
2. Press **Open**, or click the pack's picture. Three sealed packs appear: pick one and it tears open. Nothing is opened until
   you pick, so you can still close here and keep the pack.
   (The pick is a ritual: it does not change which cards you get.)
3. The cards come out face down and lie there for a moment before the first one can be turned.
   A few things hint at what is inside:
   - **A green edge** that pulses slowly is a hint: this card may fill one of your empty Foil,
     Holo or Special slots. The hint is usually right, but not always. Now and then a glowing
     card fills nothing, and a card without the glow fills a slot. Only the turned card tells.
   - **Under each back** is the card's print tier, such as "Mass print": how scarce the card is.
   - **All the backs glow a little more** the longer you have gone without a Holo or Special.
     That is for looks only: it changes no odds.
   - **Point at a back** (or move to it with **Tab**) and it glows faintly in its treatment's
     colour: grey for Normal, blue for Foil, purple for Holo, gold for Special.
4. Flip each card, or press **Reveal all**. The cards are as large as your screen allows. The
   rarest card is often the last one, but not always.
   - A card you click glows for a moment, then turns. Every card turns in its own time. A Holo,
     a Special and any card that fills a version are held for a beat first and turn slower. From
     the second pack you open in a row the wait is shorter, and from the third the Normal
     cards turn faster. **Reveal all** turns everything fast.
   - A Foil that is new or fills a version comes with sparks, a Holo with a rainbow burst and a
     Special with a gold show. The dearer the pack, the bigger the effect.
5. Under each card you see, first, **what the copy is worth** on the market. The number counts up
   and throws coins: a 6-coin card ticks, a 600-coin card bursts. Under it is what the pull did:
   - **New card:** you didn't own it yet.
   - **Fills Foil slot** (or Holo, or Special): the card fills one of your empty versions.
     A new card that does so reads "New card · Fills Foil slot".
   - **First Normal copy:** a Normal of a card you only held in a rarer treatment. Normal is the
     base and is not a version.
   - **Duplicate:** you already have this card in this treatment. You keep it as an extra copy, to
     sell or to merge. See [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
   - **Already owned — slot still empty:** a duplicate of a card that still has an empty version
     slot above the treatment you pulled. Only on a Foil or Holo you already had; a Normal duplicate just says *Duplicate*.
   - **Almost a Foil!** (or "Almost a Holo!") means the roll landed just short of the next
     treatment. It only shows on Normal and Foil cards: a Holo or a Special is already a win.
6. **The glowing border** around a turned card has the colour of that line: **pink** for a new
   card, **green** for a new version, **yellow** for a duplicate. It is light on a Normal and
   stronger on a Foil, a Holo and a Special.
7. **When a card fills a version** you hear a short sound of its own, a ring closes around the
   card, "+1 version" appears on it, and the version pill at the top left of the screen and the
   line above the cards go up by one at that moment.
8. **Click a turned card to open its panel** on top of the pack: your versions, the tags and the
   price history. Click the large card in the panel to see it **full screen**. Close the panel
   (**Esc**) and the pack is as you left it.
9. **The version line** above the cards counts along as you turn them: "No new versions yet",
   then **"+1 version"**, **"+2 versions"**, with the slots that filled under it ("Foil
   Card #000412"). It never runs ahead of the cards. When every card is turned it is final:
   **"+2 versions"** or **"No new versions"**. Under the cards is then a summary of the pack and
   what it is worth together. A milestone the pack reached is announced now; you claim its packs
   on Home.
10. Up to three prompts can appear under the summary. They show what these cards make possible
   right now:
   - **Merge 3 Normal → Foil: (card name)** when you now hold enough copies of a card (three for
     most cards, more for a valuable one). One tap merges them right there. When the merge would
     empty a version slot, the app asks first.
   - **(card name) is wanted: sells for 🪙 31 instead of 24** when a collector wants a card you
     just pulled. It opens the card's panel, where you sell it. Only cards worth 20 coins or more
     are wanted.
11. The main button under the cards is the next pack: **Open another** (while you have sealed
   packs), or **Buy and open another** (when you can afford one), or **Claim your free pack** (when
   today's free pack is ready; it takes you to **Packs**). With none of these it reads **Tag these
   cards to earn coins**. Next to it is **Close**.

**Reduced motion.** When your system asks for less motion, nothing pulses, shakes or flies: the
green edge is still, the border is a plain coloured line, "+1 version" fades in place and every
card turns at the same speed. The short waits before a card turns stay, only shorter. All
numbers, texts and sounds are the same.

**Sound.** Every sound is short, and one switch turns all of them off: **Turn sound off** on your
**Player** page. The app remembers it.

**Open N** opens several sealed packs in a row, one tap each. When you hold 10 or more sealed packs
of a kind, **Open 10** is the main button.

**The Packs button** in the bottom bar shows a number: your sealed packs. It shows **free** when you
have none and today's free pack is ready. Hold the pointer over it to see how many packs your coins
buy.

**Prices** stay the same however many packs you buy: Standard 100, Deluxe 180, Premium 480. The shop
always shows today's price. If you are short, the tile says by how much and links to the ways to earn.

**The deal.** Every 4 hours one pack is 20% to 40% cheaper. You can buy it at that price twice.
The deal is at the top of the shop, with a clock, and on Home.

**The same odds for every pack.** A pack's odds do not change with what you opened before. (Before
version 0.9.0 the odds of a Holo or Special rose after fifteen packs without one.) The glow on a
face-down card is a hint, and it can be wrong; the rarest card is not always the last one.

**Rarer cards pay more.** Tagging a card pays coins times its treatment multiplier. Pull a rarer
copy of a card you already tagged and you can review it for a bonus.
