Welcome! This app turns your personal images and videos into a **collectible card game**.

1. **Add media.** On the setup screen, press **Choose folder…** and pick the folder with your photos and videos. Every file becomes a card, hidden inside the packs.
2. **Open packs.** Buy a pack with your welcome coins and open it. The cards you pull are yours: they appear in your **Collection**.
3. **Tag your cards.** Tag the cards you pulled, one by one or in bulk. Every tag earns coins. See [Tagging and categories](#help:tagging).
4. **Buy more packs** with those coins, and chase the rare Foil, Holo and Special copies. Every card has a market value; sell or merge the copies you pull twice. See [The card market](#help:card-market).
5. **Come back daily.** A daily check-in gives you a free pack, and three small goals a day pay a pack each. See [Every day](#help:daily-and-goals).

**The top bar** shows your coins, your versions ("12 / 720 versions": the Foil, Holo and Special copies you hold, of three for every card) and what your collection is worth. The **✉** button opens your messages, such as an offer from a collector.

New here? Read [Your first session](#help:first-steps) or [take the welcome tour](#tour:welcome). The side bar of **Home**, **To claim**, holds everything you can claim: the daily check-in, the free pack, today's goals and a **Getting started** checklist; two of its steps pay a pack. Every reward is a sealed pack. Coins come from tagging and from new files.

Until you add media, the app shows only the setup screen. See [Why do I only see the setup screen?](#help:setup-screen)
