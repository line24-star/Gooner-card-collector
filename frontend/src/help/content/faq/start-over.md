---
title: How do I start over?
category: Basics
order: 35
---
Open your **Player** page (👤) and scroll to **Start over**. Press **Reset my game…** and type
`RESET` to confirm.

**Wiped:** your coins and their history, your sealed and opened packs, the cards you pulled, your
tags, your streak, the getting-started list, the tours, today's and this week's goals, your
collector level and the market's history.

**Kept:** your photos and videos, your media folder, your name and the names you gave cards. Nothing has to be imported again.

You start again with the welcome coins, and every card gets a new print run in a new market.
This can't be undone.
