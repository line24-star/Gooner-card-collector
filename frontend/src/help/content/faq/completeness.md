---
title: How is completeness counted, and why isn't my collection 100% complete?
category: Collection
order: 30
---
The pill in the top bar counts your **versions**: "187 / 720 versions". **Completeness** is the
same count as a percentage. You find it on **Stats**, on your **Player** page and in the pill's
tooltip.

**Completeness counts versions.** Every card in your folder has three versions: **Foil**, **Holo**
and **Special**. A version counts while you hold at least one copy of it.

| You hold of a card | Versions that count |
|---|---|
| Nothing, or only Normal copies | 0 of 3 |
| A Foil | 1 of 3 |
| A Foil and a Holo | 2 of 3 |
| A Foil, a Holo and a Special | 3 of 3 |

Completeness is the versions you hold, out of three for every card. With 200 cards there are 600
versions. If you hold 150 of them, your completeness is 25%. **Stats** and your **Player** page show the
count ("150 of 600 versions held").

- **Normal copies don't count.** Normal is the base: you merge from it.
- **Tags don't count.** The number of tagged cards is shown separately.
- **Several copies of one version count once.** Five Foils of a card are one version.
- **Only what you hold now counts.** A version you had and sold does not.

So 100% means you hold every card as a Foil, a Holo and a Special at the same time. Every card as
a Foil only is 33.3%. Every card as a Foil and a Holo is 66.7%. This is the long-term chase: expect
it to take months, and longer in a big library.

**Why did my completeness go down?**

- **You sold or merged away your last copy of a version.** That version slot is empty again. Merging
  your last three Foils into a Holo empties the Foil slot and fills the Holo slot. The app asks
  before it does this and names the slot. Keep one copy of a version and it stays counted:
  **Sell N extras** and **Sell all duplicates** always keep one.
- **You took a collector's offer for your only copy** of a Foil, Holo or Special. The offer says so:
  "Accepting empties the Foil slot (−1 version)".
- **You added photos or videos.** Every new file is a new card with three empty version slots, so
  the percentage drops a little. A scan that adds files tells you before it starts: "Adding 12
  media will add 36 version slots. Versions: 23% → 19%".

A file that is missing from the folder is left out on both sides: its versions don't count and
they are not asked for.

**Why did my completeness drop to 0%?**

Before version 0.7.0 a tagged card counted by its best version, and a tagged Normal card already
counted a third. Now only Foil, Holo and Special versions count. If you hold only Normal cards, you
see 0%. Nothing was taken from you: your cards, copies, tags and coins are as they were. Versions
you held and sold earlier are not counted.

**Milestones** pay sealed packs when completeness passes 1%, 2%, 5% and so on, and you claim them
yourself: a milestone you reached waits until you press **Claim**, also when your completeness
falls again. Each one pays once.

Home says how many of your cards are still Normal. Your **collector level** counts the same chase
in points and pays a pack for each level. See
