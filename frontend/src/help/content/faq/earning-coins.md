---
title: How do I earn coins?
category: Economy
order: 10
---
Coins come from **scanning and tagging**, and from selling on the market. Everything else pays
**sealed packs**, not coins.

- **Tag the cards you pulled.** The first tag on a card pays the most (30 coins); the next three
  pay 10, 5 and 2. Rarer treatments multiply that. After that, every tag pays 1 coin, up to 12 paid
  tags per card. Only cards you pulled from a pack can be tagged. One tag on each card of a pack
  pays for the next Standard pack.
- **Tag one card at a time.** In **Organize**, nine quick tags sit on the keys **1** to **9**. A tag placed on many cards at once in the Collection (**Select**) pays a quarter: bulk tagging is for sorting, not for earning.
- **Use your tag rush.** Once a day, start it in **Organize**: tags pay double for 10 minutes. Organize says when the next one is ready.
- **Review shinier cards.** When you pull a rarer version of a card you had already tagged, a **Review · +N** button appears. It pays once per card and rarity.
- **Add new media.** New files pay once each when you scan the folder: 20 coins for an image, 30 for a video. The first 5 a day pay full, the next 10 pay half, then nothing until tomorrow (at most 200 coins a day).
  Your first import doesn't pay, and neither does moving or renaming a file you already have.
- **Sell copies on the market**, or take a collector's offer (see below).

**What pays packs.** These go into **Your packs**, sealed, and cost nothing:

Claim them in the side bar of **Home**, under **To claim**, unless a line says otherwise.

- **Check in daily.** The check-in on **Home** pays no coins. It keeps your streak and unlocks a **free pack** every day.
- **Daily goals.** Once you have bought 5 packs, three small goals appear every day on **Home** and **Stats**. Each pays 1 Standard pack. Press **Claim** when a goal's bar is full.
- **Weekly goals.** Once you have bought 30 packs, three goals appear each week (like "Pull 10 Foil-or-better cards"). Each pays 1 Deluxe pack. Claim them on **Stats**.
- **Collector level.** Rarer cards and merges raise it. Each new level pays 1 Standard pack, every 5th level a Deluxe pack instead, every 10th a Premium pack. See [What is my collector level?](#help:collector-level)
- **Sets.** Own every card in a tag as Foil, Holo or Special: a Standard, Deluxe or Premium pack (two for a set of 15 cards or more). Claim it on **Stats**.
- **Milestones.** When your collection completeness passes 1%, 2%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and so on, up to 100%, a milestone waits to be claimed: 1 Standard pack for the first ones, up to 5 Premium packs at 100%. Opening a pack pays nothing by itself: press **Claim** on the milestone row under your collector level, on **Home** or **Stats**. Completeness counts the Foil, Holo and Special versions you hold, so it rises with packs and merging, not with tags. Each milestone pays once. A milestone is counted against the largest library you ever had, so taking files out of your folder does not bring one closer. See [How is completeness counted?](#help:completeness)
- See [Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets](#help:daily-and-goals).
- **Organize fast.** [Tagging and categories](#help:tagging) covers Organize mode and bulk tagging.
- **Sell copies on the market.** A duplicate from a pack is kept as an extra copy and pays nothing by itself. Sell extra copies for 85% of the card's price; cards that collectors want sell for 30% more. Selling does not lower the price: ten copies pay ten times what one pays. See [The card market](#help:card-market).
- **Take a collector's offer.** A few times a day a collector offers coins for one of your best cards. The offer slides in as a message and waits behind the **✉** button in the top bar.

You can also **spend** coins on the cards the market has for sale: up to 10 of each treatment. Merging copies into a rarer one costs nothing, and it is the one way to make a card rarer yourself (there are no upgrade attempts any more). A merge takes three copies for most cards and up to seven for the most valuable ones: see [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).

**Out of coins?** Tag the cards you pulled: one tag a card pays for the next pack. When no tag pays any more and you have no spare copies, you can still sell a card you hold on the **Market** page, scan new files, or open the packs your goals paid. Tomorrow a free pack comes with the daily check-in.

Your **Player** page lists every coin you earned and spent. See [Where is my player page?](#help:player-page)

Re-tagging the same card never pays twice. Removing a tag (click it, in Organize or in the card panel) takes back the coins it paid, and adding a tag again pays at most what was taken back.

**Tagging very fast?** After a run of very fast quick tags the page asks you to slow down for a few seconds: type the next tag, or wait. A typed tag is always paid; a quick tag sent during that short pause pays nothing. See [Tagging and categories](#help:tagging).
