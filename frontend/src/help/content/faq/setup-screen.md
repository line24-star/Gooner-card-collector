---
title: Why do I only see the setup screen?
category: Basics
order: 15
---
The app has no cards yet. Cards are made from your own photos and videos, so until at least one
is imported there is nothing to collect, and every page leads back to the setup screen.

To get going:
1. Press **Choose folder…** and pick the folder that holds your photos and videos. The normal
   Windows folder window opens, and the folder is scanned right away. Or copy files into the
   folder shown on the screen and press **Scan folder**.
2. Wait for the count under **Import** to go up.
3. Press **Start playing**.

Your coins are real from the first second: the top bar shows your welcome coins, not a demo
amount.

The same screen comes back if your media folder becomes empty or unavailable, for example when
its drive is unplugged. Plug it in again, or choose another folder. See
[How do I change my media folder?](#help:change-media-folder) and [Adding media](#help:adding-media).
