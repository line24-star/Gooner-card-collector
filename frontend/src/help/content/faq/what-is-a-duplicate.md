---
title: What is a duplicate, and what do I do with it?
category: Collection
order: 35
---
A **duplicate** is a card from a pack that you already own in that same treatment. A treatment you
hold no copy of is not a duplicate, even when you own a rarer one: a Foil, Holo or Special you did
not hold is a **new version**, and a Normal you did not hold is a **first Normal copy**.

You keep it as an **extra copy**. It pays no coins by itself. Open the card and look at
**Your versions**:

- **Sell** extra copies on the market for 85% of the card's price.
- **Merge** three copies of one treatment into one copy of the next treatment. A card that is
  worth more takes more copies, up to seven. The row says how far you are: "2 of 3 for a Foil".

In a pack, a duplicate says **Already owned** when you hold that treatment already and the card
still has an empty version slot above it.

See [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
