---
title: What is there to do when every card is pulled and tagged?
category: Economy
order: 40
---
Less than before. While you were pulling and tagging, every pack held new cards and every first tag
paid well. From here the game is slower. Expect a few minutes of play a day, not the long sessions of the early
game.

What is left:

- **Today's goals.** Three small goals a day for a Standard pack each. You only get goals you can
  still work on, such as opening packs, merging copies, or selling on the market.
- **Weekly goals.** Three bigger ones every Monday, on **Stats**.
- **Your collector level.** It rises with every Foil, Holo and Special and with every merge, and
  each level pays a pack. It has no last level.
  See [What is my collector level?](#help:collector-level)
- **Missing versions.** Every card has three version slots: Foil, Holo and Special. The pill in the
  top bar counts the ones you hold ("187 / 720 versions"); click it for the cards that still have an
  empty slot. Normal copies and tags don't count, so with every card pulled and tagged as a Normal
  you hold 0 versions. Each version you fill raises the count, and the milestones on the way pay
  sealed packs. Packs still give you duplicates to merge and, now and then, a rarer copy. Home lists the
  cards that lack exactly one version. See
  [What are versions, and what is the ring on a card?](#help:versions-and-ring) and
  [How is completeness counted?](#help:completeness)
- **Sets.** Own every card of a tag as Foil, then Holo, then Special, for a bonus each time.
- **The market.** Prices change every 6 hours. Sell duplicates, sell wanted cards for 30% more,
  take a collector's offer, or buy a copy that fills one of your empty version slots.
- **More tags.** A card pays for up to 12 tags, 1 coin each after the fourth. **Organize** offers
  the cards that still pay.
- **The daily check-in and the free pack.**

The tile **Your next goal** on Home always names the one goal that is closest.

New photos and videos bring the early game back for a while: every new file is a new card to pull
and tag. See [Adding media](#help:adding-media).

See also [Every day: check-in, free pack, goals and sets](#help:daily-and-goals) and
[Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
