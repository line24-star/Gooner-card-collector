---
title: Why does my card have a different name than the file?
category: Collection
order: 40
---
Many downloaded files have names that mean nothing, such as `GKtwUBnXcAAQeLp` or `IMG_1234`.
So a card is not named after its file:

1. **Your own name**, if you gave the card one.
2. **"Card" and the card's number** for every other card: "Card #000412".

Before version 0.9.0 a file name that read like words became the card's name. Now every card is a
numbered card until you name it.

**Every card has a number.** A card gets a six-digit number such as **#000412** when its file is
first scanned, and keeps it for good. The card panel always shows it next to the name. Cards that
were scanned before version 0.8.0 got their numbers in the order they were scanned.

**Tags do not make names.** Before version 0.8.0 a card with a meaningless file name was named
after its first tag ("Beach #0412"), so its name changed when you added or removed a tag. Now the
name stays the same whatever you tag.

**Rename a card.** Open the card and press **✎** next to its name. Type a name (up to 60
characters) and press **Save**. Leave the field empty and save to get the automatic name back.

**Your files are never renamed.** Only the card gets the name. When the two differ, the card panel
shows the real file name under the card's name. Sorting by name uses the card's name.

Names you gave your cards stay when you [start over](#help:start-over).
