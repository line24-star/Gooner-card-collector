---
title: Why can't I pull some cards from a Standard pack?
category: Collection
order: 40
---
The scarcest cards are only in the dearer packs:

- **Limited** cards (200 to 600 copies printed, about 5% of your cards) are only in **Deluxe** and
  **Premium** packs.
- **Chase** cards (25 to 100 copies printed, about 1% of your cards) are only in **Premium** packs.

Every other card is in every pack. In the shop, a pack's tile says how many cards are only in it
and how many of those you still miss. A card's panel says "Only in Premium Packs" (or "Only in
Deluxe Packs and up") where it applies.

Scarce cards are also pulled less often than common ones, in any pack. The **Market** sells the 10
most valuable cards of each treatment, so a scarce card is often for sale there too.
See [The card market](#help:card-market).
