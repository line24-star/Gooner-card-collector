---
title: How do I see a tour again?
category: Basics
order: 30
---
Open **Help** (or press `?`), go to the **Tours** tab, and click **Replay** next to any tour.
Or [start the welcome tour now](#tour:welcome).

Tours start by themselves the first time you visit **Organize**, **Packs**, **Market** or a card's
panel (after setup and the welcome tour). Each one runs once. When you have taken all five
(Welcome, Organize, Packs, Market and card panel) to their last step, you get 1 Standard pack, once. A skipped tour does not count until you finish it, and replaying never
pays again.
