---
title: What is my collector level?
category: Economy
order: 30
---
Your **collector level** is a second progress number next to completeness. It rises when your cards
get rarer and when you merge copies. Completeness counts every Foil, Holo and Special version you hold; the
collector level counts only the best copy of each card, and every merge. You find it on **Home** and on **Stats**, under **Today's goals**.

**How you get points**

| What | Points |
|---|---|
| A card whose best copy is Normal | 0 |
| A card whose best copy is a Foil | 4 |
| A card whose best copy is a Holo | 12 |
| A card whose best copy is a Special | 30 |
| Every merge you have ever done | 2 |

Only the best copy of a card counts, not how many copies you hold. Normal cards give nothing, so
pulling and tagging alone does not raise your level.

Example: you merge three Normal copies of a card into its first Foil (a valuable card takes more
copies, see [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing)). That is 6 points: 2 for the
merge and 4 because the card's best copy is now a Foil.

**Levels.** Level 1 needs 10 points, level 2 needs 30, level 3 needs 58, level 4 needs 92, level 5
needs 131 and level 10 needs 398. The levels get further apart. There is no last level: every merge
still adds points.

**Packs.** Each level pays once: 1 Standard pack. Every 5th level pays a Deluxe pack instead and
every 10th a Premium pack. Press **Claim** next to your level. One claim gives the packs of every
level you have reached.

**Selling can lower your points**, when the best copy of a card goes. Packs you were given stay
yours, and a level never pays twice. A level you reached but did not claim before your points
dropped can be claimed when you reach it again.

A merge is what the goals call a fuse: "Fuse 1 card" is done by one merge.

See also [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing) and
