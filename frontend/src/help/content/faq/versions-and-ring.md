---
title: What are versions, and what is the ring on a card?
category: Collection
order: 28
---
Every card in your folder has three **versions**: **Foil**, **Holo** and **Special**. Each one is a
**version slot**. A slot is **filled** while you hold at least one copy of that treatment, and
**empty** when you hold none. Normal is the base of a card and is not a version.

**The pill in the top bar** counts them: "187 / 720 versions" means you hold 187 of the 720 slots
(three for each of 240 cards).

- After a pack, a merge, a sale, a purchase or a scan the pill shows the change for 2 seconds:
  "+2", "−1", or "+36 slots" after a scan that added 12 files.
- A gain pops in green. A loss fades in the colour of the treatment whose slot emptied.
- Hold the pointer over the pill for the percentage and for how many cards you pulled.
- **Click the pill** to open the Collection with **Missing versions** on.

**The ring on a card.** Every card carries a small ring of three segments, clockwise from the top:
Foil, Holo, Special. A filled slot is a thick segment in that treatment's colour (blue, purple,
gold). An empty slot is a thin outline. Hold the pointer over the ring to read it in words
("Foil held · Holo missing · Special missing").

**Missing versions.** In the Collection, the chip **Missing versions** lists the cards you pulled
that still have an empty slot. The cards closest to holding all three come first, then the most
valuable. The line above the list links to the packs: **Open packs to fill versions**.

**Missing one version.** Home lists up to 10 cards that lack exactly one version, the most valuable
first, with the name of the treatment that is missing.

**In the binder.** Switch on **Versions** next to the page buttons: each spread shows one card in
four pockets, Normal, Foil, Holo and Special. A version you do not hold is a dashed frame that says
"Not owned". Click it to see a preview of that version full screen.

**How a slot fills**

- **A pack.** A green edge on a face-down card is a hint that it may fill a slot. The hint is
  usually right, but not always: some glowing cards fill nothing, and some cards without the glow
  fill a slot. When a card that fills a slot is turned, it says so ("Fills Foil slot"), you hear a
  short sound, "+1 version" appears on the card and the pill goes up by one.
- **A merge.** Several copies of one treatment merge into one copy of the next.
  See [Duplicates and merging](#help:duplicates-and-fusing).
- **The market.** Buying a Foil, Holo or Special copy fills its slot. The listing says
  "✚ Fills your Holo slot" when it does. See [The card market](#help:card-market).

**How a slot empties.** Selling, merging away or handing a collector your last copy of a Foil, Holo
or Special empties its slot. The app asks first and says so: "This will empty the Foil slot
(−1 version)". Keep one copy and the slot stays filled.
