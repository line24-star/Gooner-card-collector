---
title: When do prices change?
category: Economy
order: 20
---
**Every 6 hours, for all cards at the same moment:** at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC. The
**Market** page counts down to the next update and shows the four times in your own clock.

- **While the app is closed** nothing is lost. When you open it again, the updates you missed are
  played back (up to 120 days), so prices, news and price charts are as if the app had run.
- **Between two updates** a price stands still. **Your own trades do not move it:** you are one
  collector among millions. Selling ten copies pays ten times what one copy pays, and a card costs
  the same after you bought it as before. (Before version 0.8.0 each copy you sold lowered the
  price by 3%.)
- **After a hype or a buyout** a card is on a run for a few updates. At each one it rises again
  (6 times in 10, in smaller steps) or drops back a good part. The two cancel out on average, so
  the news gives no sure gain.
- **The ▲ or ▼ in the top bar** shows how much your collection went up or down at the last update.
  It compares the cards you hold now at the last update with the same cards at the update before.
- **Your collection's value** counts the cards you hold now. It also changes when you open packs,
  sell or buy.

Wanted cards and an offer from a collector last until the next update.
