title: Where is my player page?
category: Basics
order: 18
---
Press the **👤** button in the top bar, or **Player** in the navigation.

It shows:
- **Your name.** Type a new one and press **Save**.
- **Your numbers:** coins (with everything you ever earned and spent), sealed packs, cards
  pulled and collection completeness (the percentage, how many Foil, Holo and Special versions you
  hold, and how many of your cards are tagged).
- **Media folder:** where your cards come from, with **Scan for new media**, **Open folder** and
  **Change folder…**. The app only looks at the folder when you press a button.
- **Coin history:** totals per source (such as "Tagging" or "Card sold on the market"), and
  your latest 30 coin changes with the balance after each.
- **App:** sound on or off, replay a tour, **Show the getting-started list** (if you hid it),
  Help, and **Open the log folder**.
- **Start over:** **Reset my game…** wipes your progress and keeps your photos and videos.
  See [How do I start over?](#help:start-over)

There is one local player and no sign-in. Everything stays on this computer.

**Logs.** The app writes what it does to `data/logs/server.log`: one file per day, and files
older than two days are deleted by themselves. If something goes wrong, that file says what
happened. Errors from the browser page are written there too.
